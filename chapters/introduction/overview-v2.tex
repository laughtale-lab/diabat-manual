\section{Overview}
\progname{} is a diabatization toolkit developed independently by Yu-Chen Wang for research in theoretical and computational chemistry. Its principal purpose is to construct quasi-diabatic electronic states and calculate the electronic couplings between them. Several diabatization methods are available for problems involving charge transfer, excitation energy transfer, and processes in which different electronic-state characters coexist. Orbital and electronic-state analyses are also available to assist with preparation and interpretation of calculations.

The official repository is \url{https://github.com/laughtale-lab/diabat}. This manual describes version 2.0 of \progname{}.
